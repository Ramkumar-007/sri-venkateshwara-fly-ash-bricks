How the bricks are made

Six steps from fly ash to your site.

  1. Source

    Fly ash, crusher dust, crusher waste and cement are brought in as raw materials.

  2. Mix

    Blended in set proportions for each batch.

  3. Mould

    Pressed into bricks of one standard size.

  4. Cure

    Left to gain strength before use.

  5. Check

    Bricks are inspected for size and finish.

  6. Dispatch

    Loaded and sent to your site.

Questions builders ask

How strong are fly ash bricks compared with red bricks?

Strength depends on the mix and the batch. Ask us for the test report of the bricks you are buying and compare it with what your engineer specifies.

Will I use less plaster?

Fly ash bricks have smooth, even faces and a consistent size, so walls come out flatter and usually need less plaster to level.

Do the bricks need curing on site?

Bricks are cured at our plant before dispatch. Your mason can ask us about wetting and laying practice when you order.
